Our Story

A casual craft beer bar serving an Indian street food feast

Hidden on 6 Mill Hill in the heart of Leeds, Bundobust brings together the buzz of Indian street food and the craft of British brewing. Bare brick walls, communal wooden benches, and a menu built for sharing.

Every dish is vegetarian — and with a huge range of vegan options, the flavours never miss. From the legendary Okra Fries to the Bundobust Chipa and Mango Lassi, everything is cooked fresh and packed with spice.

Whether you're here for a quick bite or a long session over craft beer, the vibe is lively, friendly, and reasonably priced.
